Correct the regression without reopening the 45-minute role policy.

The pi-subagents vendor runtime, unrelated Observer surfacing, the focus-independent intake prerequisite, T-164, new tools, persistent state, gates, and confinement grants are outside this Change. Operator approval and merge authority remain unchanged.

Decision ledger:

- `doc-116` and the asked-and-answered operator handoff on 2026-07-27 (“Alright, let's go ahead and do that to hand it off.”) — initial implementer/reviewer/researcher launches omit call-site timeout overrides so manifests own policy; research becomes a normal top-level single launch; recovery reuses the source run's recorded timeout; tests prohibit Skill role-timeout literals and retain all four 2700000ms manifests.
- `doc-116` and the same operator handoff — keep this Change qq-only and separate from Observer surfacing, focus-independent intake, and T-164; add no tools, durable runtime state, gates, or confinement grants; do not merge.
- `doc-116` and the asked-and-answered 2026-07-27 review realignment, where the operator selected “Derive from manifest” — `qq-dispatch` derives its canonical delegate containment timeout from the exact trusted role manifest instead of retaining an independent 30-minute default; the pi-subagents vendor runtime remains unchanged.
