## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Build the complete qq-owned operator-alignment and voice interface through three permanent, dependency-ordered Changes. A privileged translator/aligner is the visible accountable project-home Pi and the operator's sole operational interface during a Change. A fully internal orchestrator and other work Actors supply briefs, findings, evidence, and escalation material without addressing the operator directly. The aligner requests missing material, translates in both directions, and uses complementary speech, visual presentation, diagrams, pages, and other explanatory artifacts while preserving provenance and the full trace. The architect remains a deliberate, operator-invoked independent audit surface after the fact.

Decision ledger:
- Build one target system through permanent dependency-ordered increments, with no throwaway prototype, temporary role topology, panel/popup alternative, or alternate handoff format — approved asked-and-answered operator alignment exchange.
- Operator alignment is a distinct concern with three episodes: initial alignment, criteria-triggered realignment, and acceptance — same exchange.
- The privileged root aligner is the sole operational interface; orchestration is fully internal, work Actors do not address the operator, and their traces remain inspectable evidence — same exchange, superseding the earlier delegated-aligner recommendation.
- Observer directly audits preserved work/alignment traces; the Architect remains directly available to synthesize and selectively route or set aside cited Observer findings — decision-18 and operator-approved asked-and-answered realignment, 2026-07-27.
- The aligner translates and aligns from material produced by responsible Actors, may resolve supplied citations and request missing data, and does not independently investigate, implement, validate, dispatch work, mutate delivery state, or exercise operator authority — same exchange.
- The aligner may create derived explanatory artifacts in any useful medium, but they remain orientation rather than source evidence and preserve provenance — same exchange.
- Input/transcription uses OpenAI gpt-4o-transcribe; output/speech uses Google gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview; Gemini voice identity and direction/tuning remain open for the voice-channel Change — explicit operator context update.
- Delivery uses one parent Task and three child Tasks/Changes in the approved order: alignment core, voice channels, persistent calibration — approved asked-and-answered operator alignment exchange.
