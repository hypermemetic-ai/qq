## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Build qq's permanent operator-alignment foundation around a privileged, visible aligner and a fully internal orchestrator. The aligner is the operator's sole operational interface during a Change; the orchestrator and work Actors provide briefs, findings, evidence, and escalation material without addressing the operator directly. The aligner may resolve supplied citations, demand missing material, translate in both directions, and create visual/explanatory presentations while preserving provenance and the full trace. The existing architect remains a deliberate, operator-invoked independent audit surface after the fact and may identify gaps in the work or alignment process itself.

Decision ledger:
- Expand T-152's canonical catalog from six roles to seven by adding aligner; all previously settled role semantics remain unchanged except the root occupancy and interaction topology explicitly superseded below — approved full-system alignment exchange.
- Supersede the earlier delegated-aligner recommendation: the visible accountable project-home Pi is the privileged aligner; the orchestrator is fully internal and never addresses the operator directly — asked-and-answered operator alignment exchange.
- The aligner is the sole operational conversational interface during a Change; internal Actor traces remain inspectable evidence rather than competing interaction surfaces — same exchange.
- Observer directly audits preserved work/alignment traces; the Architect remains a direct, operator-invoked synthesis and selective-routing surface over cited Observer findings — decision-18 and operator-approved asked-and-answered realignment, 2026-07-27.
- The aligner's capability boundary is broad epistemic and presentational access, narrow typed coordination with the orchestrator, and no execution or operator-disposition authority — same exchange.
- The aligner may open exact supplied evidence and trace references, but may not broadly investigate the Repository; citation resolution belongs to alignment, while discovery and substantive validation return to the internal orchestrator — same exchange.
- The aligner may request missing analysis/evidence, translate and clarify, use the glossary, create derived explanatory artifacts, and carry the operator's exact disposition back; it may not run shell/Checks, mutate Repository or delivery state, dispatch work Actors, resolve technical/product choices, or act for the operator — same exchange.
- Replace rather than append the aligner root system prompt and launch it with exact extensions, no inherited project context/Skills/templates, and an exact active-tool set; coding and delivery methodology belong only to the internal orchestrator — approved topology exchange.
- Run one session-long trusted orchestrator as a depth-1 fanout child; its existing work roles remain depth-2 trusted descendants. A qq-owned typed broker, not generic intercom or the prose-only native supervisor channel, carries aligner/orchestrator exchanges, evidence capabilities, dispositions, and trace receipts — approved topology exchange and verified research doc-114.
- The orchestrator's outer Landstrip policy may write only qq's Change-worktree root, Git common state, and private runtime state needed by writable descendants; primary main remains non-writable and every nested worker retains its narrower inner policy — explicit operator approval after criteria-triggered security realignment.
- Risk that the aligner could disappear into a long orchestrator exchange is acknowledged; add no speculative loop cap or intervention mechanism until observation proves the failure occurs — explicit operator disposition.
- Calibration state is not material the aligner may inspect; Observer audits completed traces, the Architect later synthesizes resulting findings into calibration proposals, the operator disposes them, and the calibration system applies accepted behavior — decision-18 and explicit operator disposition.
- Build the final core directly, with no panel/popup alternative, manual prototype, temporary topology, alternate handoff format, or disposable text-only system — approved permanent delivery sequence and privileged-interface disposition.
- Derived diagrams, pages, and other presentations are orientation rather than evidence and must retain provenance to supplied material — asked-and-answered design exchange.
- Treat the trusted orchestrator as a fallible evidence proposer, never as broker authority: the shared channel is untrusted transport; the root broker mechanically validates and promotes evidence into root-only authoritative state. Use the exact installed lifecycle contract and fail closed without proven terminal state; admit only the canonical Change-worktree root rather than discovered linked worktrees. Primary-main path guards remain defense-in-depth for trusted Actors, not a hostile-process isolation claim — operator-approved convergence-breaker realignment after doc-117.
