## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Build qq's permanent operator-alignment foundation around a privileged visible aligner and one fully internal session-long orchestrator. The aligner is the operator's sole operational interface during a Change; the orchestrator and work Actors provide bounded facts, implications, recommendations, source-referenced material, and escalation packets without addressing the operator directly. The aligner requests missing material, translates in both directions, creates complementary spoken/visual explanations and temporary presentation artifacts, and preserves exact operator dispositions without performing Change work.

The permanent root/orchestrator topology and complete lifecycle remain in T-165.1. Supplied material is carried inline in closed bounded packets rather than exposed through raw-path evidence capabilities. Native root and child Pi session JSONL plus qq custom entries/tool-result state are the sole content and operational continuity seam; T-165.1 adds no parallel content journal, sealing protocol, or sealed package. Current Observer/Architect runtime, package, digest, intake, launcher, profile, and Skill behavior remain unchanged; decision-18 records their broader role split but T-165.1 does not implement a new audit surface.

Decision ledger:
- Add aligner as qq's seventh canonical role; preserve all previously settled role semantics except the approved root occupancy/topology — approved full-system alignment exchange.
- The visible project-home Pi is the privileged aligner and sole operational conversational interface; one session-long internal orchestrator owns execution and depth-2 work-role fanout — asked-and-answered operator alignment and topology exchanges.
- Keep the permanent first increment, full internal-orchestrator lifecycle/session-replacement obligations, and integration with moving landed source; these are not scope defects — explicit operator scope realignment, 2026-07-27: “1, 2 and 6 -- don't seem like valid criticism to me.”
- Scale down the accepted scope defects: remove raw evidence-capability machinery, the parallel authoritative content journal/sealing/package system, and T-165.1-owned Architect/Observer runtime integration — same explicit operator disposition: “The rest of your points though, absolutely. So I say we scale down on all of those.”
- The aligner receives only bounded inline supplied material and source references; inadequate material returns `needs-data`. It receives no path opening, search, capability minting, Repository investigation, execution, mutation, dispatch, delivery, calibration, or operator-disposition authority — operator's original translator/aligner vision and 2026-07-27 scope realignment.
- Preserve content and reconstruct operational continuity through native Pi session JSONL, strict tool-result/custom entries, shared correlation ids, and Pi session replacement semantics; no second content truth surface — decision-10 plus 2026-07-27 scope realignment.
- Observer directly audits persisted sessions under the existing post-hoc system; Architect continues existing cited-findings synthesis/routing behavior. Do not add a new Architect root or alignment-specific Observer package in this Change — decision-18 plus 2026-07-27 scope realignment.
- Replace rather than append the root aligner prompt; load exact extensions/resources/tools and fail closed on drift. Coding/delivery methodology belongs to the orchestrator — approved topology exchange.
- The internal orchestrator launches as a trusted process without an outer Landstrip boundary; the dispatcher still validates its declared governed checkout/resources, and actual work roles retain their narrower Landstrip policies — asked-and-answered operator simplification, 2026-07-27: “we're not doing anything that complex… are we assuming the orchestrator needs to be in a landstrip thing now? They weren't earlier and we lived perfectly fine.”
- Exact-run terminal proof is mandatory before replacement cleanup; failure retains recoverable lifecycle state and refuses overlap — approved convergence realignment, retained by the operator.
- Exact empty-root pre-spawn proof stays in the broker boundary: reuse the exact-object validator, require primitive-string status text, and accept only the pinned vendor budget-summary plus empty-run grammar; this one final parser correction is an explicit convergence-breaker waiver — asked-and-answered root-interface UAT exchange, 2026-07-27: “Approve parser (Recommended).”
- Do not add speculative anti-chatter controls until observed; calibration is applied later and is not aligner-readable — explicit operator dispositions.
- Build the final root/orchestrator/presentation foundation directly with no popup/panel/manual prototype or temporary topology; derived artifacts remain orientation with supplied-material provenance — approved permanent delivery sequence.
