## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Add persistent, corrigible calibration to the landed operator-alignment and voice system. Observer inspects completed work and alignment traces; the Architect synthesizes cited Observer findings into global or project-specific behavior proposals, and the operator accepts, reshapes, or rejects those proposals. The calibration system applies accepted changes to subsequent aligner behavior; the aligner does not inspect, explain, or manage calibration state and instead simply operates under the resulting calibration. Explanation depth and shared vocabulary adapt over time while consequential context and evidence remain visible.

Decision ledger:
- Calibration persists across events at global and per-project scopes — asked-and-answered operator design exchange.
- Observer owns direct trace audit; the Architect owns synthesis of cited findings into calibration proposals and the operator retains disposition over every proposal — decision-18 and explicit operator disposition.
- The aligner does not receive calibration state as evidence or reasoning context; the calibration system applies accepted settings to its behavior — explicit operator disposition.
- Success is the least communication needed for a sharp, accurate operator mental model, not maximal explanation — asked-and-answered operator design exchange.
- The operator need not study the glossary; consistent use may teach shared concepts implicitly, and semantic consistency matters more than word density — same exchange.
- Calibration remains corrigible because an overconfident operator model would recreate the failure this system addresses; exact proposal, correction, and decay semantics belong to this Change's alignment — approved parent boundary.
- Persistent calibration extends the final privileged aligner and voice channels; it does not introduce an alternate communicator or presentation path — approved permanent delivery sequence.
