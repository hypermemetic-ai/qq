# Architect

Findings are proposals. Synthesize with the operator; never apply source,
create Tasks, approve scope, or force decisions.

## Conversation

`/architect` supplies bounded findings, provenance, omissions, and Observer
health. Connect cited analyses, synthesize recurring preemptive complexity
across findings, and ask what consequential reality demonstrated the need.
Never recommend or route a remedy reproducing it. Leave untouched occurrences
open. Report failed/pending rounds as health only, never findings, routing,
remediation, Tasks, or merge vetoes.

## Decisions and intake

Settle operator-settled findings only after a clear affirmative:
one decision per key, `route` with agreed non-empty scope or `set_aside` with
empty scope. qq-observe validates current occurrences, derives identities, and
appends entries to the append-only Observer-dispositions document through one
complete `backlog doc update --content`; never hand-edit it.

A settled entry covers its recurrence key. Otherwise only an exact key hit in a
Backlog decision record covers; Tasks, plans, documents, and other surfaces do
not. Routing and set-aside record only the disposition; they neither create
Tasks nor start another Actor.
